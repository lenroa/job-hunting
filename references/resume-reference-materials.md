# Resume Reference Materials

Use this file when improving the user's resume, rewriting project bullets, or generating role-specific resume variants. Keep all suggestions truthful and evidence-backed.

## Strong Resume Principles

A strong product or job-search resume helps HR and interviewers quickly see:

- Who the candidate is: a clear positioning statement.
- What problems they solve: workflow efficiency, customer experience, revenue, growth, automation, data, platform, operations, or domain-specific problems.
- How they solve them: discovery, analysis, prioritization, roadmap, PRD, prototype, stakeholder alignment, launch, training, measurement, iteration.
- What changed: adoption, cost, efficiency, revenue, retention, satisfaction, quality, turnaround time, risk, or conversion.
- Why it is credible: scope, users, customers, artifacts, constraints, tradeoffs, and contribution boundary.

Prefer this structure:

```text
For [business/user problem], used [product action or method] to deliver [system/feature/process], resulting in [verified outcome].
```

## Positioning Patterns

Use only the positioning the user's evidence supports.

```text
[Domain] product manager with experience in [project type], [user group], and [business outcome].
```

```text
[Role type] focused on [problem space], with hands-on experience in [methods/tools] and [outcome].
```

Avoid unsupported positioning such as "AI platform expert", "growth owner", "data scientist", "technical architect", or "industry specialist" unless the user has confirmed evidence.

## Bullet Patterns

### Product Discovery

```text
Conducted [research method] with [user/customer group] to identify [problem], then translated findings into [requirement/roadmap/prototype], supporting [decision/outcome].
```

### B2B Or Internal System

```text
Mapped [offline/manual/fragmented workflow], designed [state flow/permission/configuration/exception handling], and launched [system/module], improving [verified metric].
```

### AI Or Automation

```text
Identified [repeatable workflow step] suitable for AI or automation, designed [workflow/evaluation/human fallback], and measured results through [metric], achieving [verified outcome].
```

### Growth Or Operations

```text
Analyzed [funnel/user segment/channel], tested [strategy], and improved [conversion/activation/retention/efficiency] from [baseline] to [result].
```

### Customer Or Solution Work

```text
Worked with [customer/stakeholder type] to diagnose [need], shaped [solution], and supported [delivery/adoption/commercial result].
```

## Resume Inclusion Criteria

Recommend adding an experience only when:

- It matches the target role or repeated JD demand.
- The user can explain the work with concrete context, decisions, tradeoffs, and results.
- It has evidence such as a metric, artifact, launch, process change, customer impact, cost/revenue impact, or adoption.
- It strengthens the target positioning instead of making the resume scattered.
- It can be worded truthfully without inflating ownership.

Do not recommend adding:

- Buzzwords learned only superficially.
- One-off tasks that cannot survive interview probing.
- Unsupported metrics or ownership.
- Confidential details the user cannot discuss.

## Rewrite Guardrails

- Use active verbs but keep ownership accurate.
- Replace vague claims with evidence.
- Put the most target-relevant projects first.
- Separate confirmed facts from questions.
- Do not add tools, metrics, industries, or responsibilities unless the user confirms them.
